# Health content safety

T1D Football Academy is an educational conversation aid for young children and their trusted adults. It is not a medical device and must not make clinical decisions.

## Content rules

Every activity must:

- direct the child to a trusted adult when something feels different or a device alerts
- use neutral language around glucose, food, insulin and devices
- make clear that type 1 diabetes is not the child's fault
- preserve adult responsibility for checking, interpreting and treating
- allow feelings such as anger, fear or frustration without penalising them

Activities must never:

- calculate, recommend or adjust insulin
- recommend carbohydrate quantities or treatment timings
- diagnose a hypo or hyper from symptoms
- label readings, foods or choices as good, bad, naughty, failed or perfect
- reward a particular glucose reading, time in range, injection or food choice
- imply that completing the game replaces the child's individual care plan
- collect or expose personal medical data

## Review requirement

Any new health statement or treatment-related scenario should be reviewed by a qualified paediatric diabetes professional before release beyond private family use. Child-specific quantities and instructions belong in the child's clinical care plan, not in source code.
